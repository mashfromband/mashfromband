# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This repository is maintained as a professional and respectful space.

Expected behavior:

- Be direct, constructive, and respectful.
- Focus feedback on the content or change.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Do not share private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

The repository owner may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributors that violate this standard.
